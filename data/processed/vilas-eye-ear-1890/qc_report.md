## This book was the wrong shape

It was carried as a **repertory** with two sections and 2,465 rubrics, marked damaged.
It is not a repertory: it is an elementary **manual**, organised by condition
(Conjunctivitis Granulosa, Glaucoma, Myopia, Otitis Chronica Media…), each with a
description and an `Internal Remedies` list, and only its final part is a
*Repertory of the Eye*.

It is now a treatise with seventeen sections, the last of which is that repertory.

## The trade, stated plainly

The 2,465 rubrics of the old entry are now prose inside the `Repertory of the Eye`
section. They are still readable and searchable as text, but they are no longer
individual rubric rows with remedy chips. That is a real loss for the app's rubric
search, accepted because the other ninety per cent of the book was unreadable in the
old shape — and because inventing a rubric structure the book does not use would be
worse than admitting the shape.

## How it was split

Chapter headings are not set larger in this scan, so height cannot find them. The
**CONTENTS page** is legible and lists every condition, and the body is split where
those titles appear. Sub-heads (`Internal Remedies`, `Miscellaneous`, `Local
Applications`) are kept inside their parent section rather than becoming chapters of
their own, and repeated titles are merged.

## Known limitations

- **Coverage is partial**: seventeen sections against roughly thirty in the contents.
  Conditions whose headings the OCR damaged are absent rather than reconstructed.
- **No grades** — this is prose, not a graded repertory.
