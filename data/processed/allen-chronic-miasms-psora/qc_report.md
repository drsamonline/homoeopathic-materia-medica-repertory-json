## How the text was obtained

This scan has **no text layer**: every page is an image. All 283 pages were rendered at
150 dpi and read with RapidOCR, at roughly eighteen seconds a page. The text is therefore
machine OCR of a 1908 printing, not a publisher's text file, and its errors are that
OCR's errors.

An Internet Archive copy of this title exists and was rejected: its title page is a
**1993 B. Jain reprint, "© Copyright with the Publisher"**. The text underneath is Allen's
and is public domain, but a modern reprint's file is not the source this library should
cite, and the original printing was available.

## Normalisation

- runs of spaces collapsed; a word broken by a line-end hyphen rejoined;
- the running head (`THE CHRONIC MIASMS.`) and bare page numbers dropped.

Nothing was summarised, reworded or corrected.

## Known limitations

- **This is OCR, and it shows.** Expect damaged words throughout — `finishhed`,
  `CHRONICMIIASMS`, `Tecth` for Teeth — because correcting them would be rewriting.
- **One source, so no corroboration.** There is nothing to check this text against, and
  the fidelity claim is only "this is what the OCR read from these pages".
- 0 index entries could not be used
  (none), so those headings sit inside the
  chapter before them.
