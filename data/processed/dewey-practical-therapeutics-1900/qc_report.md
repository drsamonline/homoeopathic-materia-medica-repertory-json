## Why the archive text

The supplied PDF of this title is **image-only**; its body pages carry no text layer. The
same scan on archive.org does have one, and that is what this volume was built from.

## How it was read

The book is organised by **disease**, each heading printed in capitals with a full stop —
`ABSCESS.`, `ACNE.`, `ADDISON'S DISEASE.` — followed by the remedies and their
indications in prose. Running heads repeat the disease name and are merged onto the first
occurrence. The publisher's imprint line was dropped rather than kept as a chapter.

## Known limitations

- **Prose, not rubric rows.** Each disease is carried whole; the remedies inside it are not
  separated into graded links, because Dewey describes rather than grades.
- **OCR damage** in headings and text carried through.
- **No corroboration** beyond the scan.
