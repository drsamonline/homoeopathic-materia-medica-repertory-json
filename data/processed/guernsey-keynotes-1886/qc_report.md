## What the book is

Brief key-notes, not a full schema: a remedy often has one general sentence and one or two
sections. Abies Canadensis, for instance, carries "The most characteristic symptoms seem to
be" and a single `Gastric. —` section. The thinness is the book's, not the parse's.

## How the parse works

A remedy opens with its name in capitals and a full stop. Inside an entry, `Head. —`,
`Gastric. —`, `Mental. —` open sections wherever they fall — this scan sets them inline
rather than at line starts, so the marker is matched anywhere rather than anchored.

## Known limitations

- **142 remedies**, and coverage is unverified: the scan's own list of remedies was not
  usable, so a remedy whose heading the OCR destroyed is absent rather than reconstructed.
- **OCR damage carried through** — "ABJCES CANADENSIS" for ABIES CANADENSIS, "Graving" for
  Craving, and the like.
- **One source, no corroboration.**
