## What it is

Margaret Tyler's *Pointers to the Common Remedies*, arranged by **disease** rather than by
remedy: apoplexy, asthma, backache, chickenpox, cholera, chorea, cough, cystitis, deafness,
dentition, diarrhoea, diphtheria and so on. Each article lists the common remedies for that
condition with the indications that separate them — "grouped for intensity or for
comparison", as her own subheading puts it.

## How it was read

One section per article, taken from the HTML as delivered. The Médi-T page furniture — the
site name, the A–Z navigation strip, the presenter line — is stripped from each, since none
of it is Tyler's.

## One rights note

The file is a **2006 compilation** ("Updated-Fixed 08-2006") of articles transcribed by
Médi-T. Tyler's text itself is public domain, and the transcription is a faithful copy
rather than a new edition; the compiler's own edits are not separable from the text, which
is why the source is recorded as the compilation rather than as a printed edition.

## Known limitations

- **Prose, not rubric rows.** Each article is carried whole; the remedies inside it are not
  separated into graded links.
- **No page numbers or edition statement** — the transcription carries none, so no edition
  is claimed in the record.
