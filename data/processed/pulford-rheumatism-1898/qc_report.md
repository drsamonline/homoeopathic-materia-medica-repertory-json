## What changed

The volume carried one section and 7,518 rubrics — the whole book in a single undivided
list. It now has the sixteen regions Pulford actually uses: Shoulders and Arms, Upper
Extremities, Scapular Region, Renal Region, Small of Back, Lumbar, Sacral, Coccygeal,
Lower Extremities, Hips, General Symptoms, Accompanying Symptoms, Ears–Nose, Appetite
and Thirst, Chill Heat and Sweat.

The headings are printed in capitals and repeat as running heads, so they are merged by
name rather than opening a chapter per page.

## A small, honest loss

Rubrics fall from 7,518 to 7,142 and links from 12,235 to 11,716 — about five per cent.
The earlier extraction was slightly more generous at the tail of some lines; the new one
is stricter about what counts as a citation. The structure gained is worth more than the
margin lost, but the difference is recorded rather than rounded away.

## Known limitations

- **One heading is OCR-mangled**: "IIA HIPS" is printed where the book says HIPS, and its
  chapter is kept under that spelling because that is what the scan says.
- **No grades** — this scan's superscripts did not survive, so `grade` is null throughout.
- **OCR damage in citations** carried through, not corrected.
