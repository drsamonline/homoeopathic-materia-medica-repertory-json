## What was recovered

The rubric text: 2,303 symptom strings, verbatim, each taken from the printed line up to
its colon. That is the substance of the book and it is intact.

## What was not

**Only about one remedy link per rubric.** Kimball cites remedies as very short
abbreviations — `Am.`, `Bar-m.`, `Aur-met.` — and the resolver needs at least three letters
in a citation's first word before it will attempt a match. Most of his citations fall below
that, so they were dropped rather than guessed at.

Lowering the threshold would start attaching wrong remedies to symptoms, which is worse for
a repertory than an empty link list. The defect is the same one that afflicts Clarke's
Clinical Repertory, and it needs the same fix: the scan re-parsed with the citation run
rejoined, not a looser matcher.

## Also

- **One chapter only** — the region headings in this scan are too few and too damaged to
  serve as divisions, so the book is carried as a single sequence rather than given chapters
  it does not clearly have.
- **No grades** — none are printed in a form this scan preserves.
