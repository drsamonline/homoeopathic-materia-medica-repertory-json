## Rights — read this before republishing

Bach died on 27 November 1936. His work is public domain in India (life + 60, expired
1996) and in the UK/EU (life + 70, expired 2007). In the **United States** a 1932
publication runs to **1 January 2028**, so it is not yet free there, and this repository is
hosted in the US. The record says so in `book.public_domain_note` and
`book.copyright_jurisdictions` rather than leaving a bare `public_domain: true` to be
misread.

## Why only this one of Bach's books

*Heal Thyself* (1931) and *The Twelve Healers* (1933) are the other two surviving texts.
Neither could be built honestly:

- the archive.org copy of *Heal Thyself* is **access-restricted lending stock**, not a
  public scan;
- the *Twelve Healers* item holds no text or PDF at all;
- the PDFs circulating freely are the **Bach Centre's electronic editions, © 2009 and
  2011**, carrying their own introduction and footnotes — a modern edited text, not
  Bach's printing.

## Structure

Twelve chapters. The book prints `CHAPTER N.` followed by the chapter's title; those heads
carry OCR damage and a dropped capital, so the titles are taken from the book's own INDEX,
which prints them cleanly, and the text from the body.

## Normalisation

- runs of spaces collapsed; words broken by a line-end hyphen rejoined (this scan uses ¬
  as the hyphen);
- bare page numbers and rules dropped.

Nothing was rewritten. The OCR's own slips are carried through.
