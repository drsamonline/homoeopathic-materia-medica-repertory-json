## Why this volume matters

The library already carried Allen's *Chronic Miasms*, which is a commentary on Hahnemann's
doctrine. It did **not** carry Hahnemann's own book. An earlier attempt at this title from a
scanned copy failed — the scan's headings came out as translator bylines ("Charles J.
Hempel, M.D") and the parse was abandoned rather than shipped.

This text arrives already transcribed, so there is no OCR damage anywhere in it.

## Structure

The book divides into six parts, taken from its own headings:

- Preface
- Nature of Chronic Diseases — 22,088 words
- Symptoms of Latent Psora — 14,751 words
- Sycosis — 1,099 words
- Syphilis — 26,032 words
- The Medicines — 4,633 words

## Known limitations

- **The theoretical part only.** Hahnemann's work runs to further volumes of materia medica
  provings; this transcription carries the doctrine, not the proving volumes.
- **The transcription's own headings are kept** — no chapter has been invented where the
  text had none.
- **No corroboration** against a scan.
