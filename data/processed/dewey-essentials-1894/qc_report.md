## Why chapters and not remedies

This book is a *quiz compend* arranged by kingdom, not a remedy-by-remedy dictionary. Its
six chapters are General Considerations, Homoeopathic Pharmacy, the Vegetable Kingdom, the
Animal Kingdom, the Nosodes, and the Mineral Kingdom — and the individual remedies sit
inside those chapters as questions and answers.

The scan's remedy headings are title-case and OCR-damaged, so keying on them would have
produced invented section names. The book's own six chapters are used instead. Chapter III
and VI are long (25,315 and 23,708 words) because that is how the book divides.

## Known limitations

- **Coarse structure.** Six sections is the book's own division, but it makes for long
  sections; the remedies inside them are not separately keyed.
- **No corroboration.** One scan, so the fidelity claim is "this is what the scan says".
- OCR damage is carried through — "GENEEAL COH8IDEEATION8" is the scan's own rendering of
  "GENERAL CONSIDERATIONS".
