## What it completes

The library already carried *Materia Medica Pura* A–I. This is Volume IV, running from
**Opium** to **Veratrum album**: Opium, Pulsatilla, Rheum, Rhus, Ruta, Sambucus nigra,
Spigelia anthelmia, Spongia marina tosta, Scilla maritima, Delphinium staphisagria, Datura
stramonium, Leontodon taraxacum, Thuya occidentalis, Veratrum album.

## How it was read

A remedy opens with its name in capitals on its own line. Hahnemann's
`OBSERVATIONS OF OTHERS.` sub-heading is kept **inside** its remedy rather than becoming an
entry of its own, because that is where it belongs in the book.

Merging is deliberately conservative — one name contained in another, or the same first and
last word — because this volume carries both botanical and short forms of the same drug
("Datura Stramonium" and "Stramonium"). Fourteen entries survive that, which is the volume's
actual remedy count.

## Known limitations

- **Prose provings, not numbered symptoms.** Each remedy is carried whole in
  `sections.general`; the numbered symptoms Hahnemann prints are inside that text rather than
  separated into rows.
- **OCR damage in headings** carried through where it survived merging — the scan gives
  "Thuya Occidentalis" for Thuya occidentalis and a mangled imprint that was removed rather
  than kept as a remedy.
- **No corroboration** beyond the scan.
