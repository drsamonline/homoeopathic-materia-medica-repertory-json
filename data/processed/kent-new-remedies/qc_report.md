## Why a transcription, not a scan

Kent's *New Remedies* exists in this library's source corpus only as typed pages, one per
remedy — the same Médi-T source that gave us Allen's Keynotes. A typed page carries no OCR
damage, which matters because these are small remedies whose names OCR badly.

This is also the readable form of a title that arrived as an unopenable iSilo `.pdb`:
`kentnewr.pdb` in the 30-books archive.

## Coverage — stated exactly

**Twenty of thirty remedies are here.** The ten absent are:

Ferrum iodatum · Hamamelis · Kali bichromicum · Kali muriaticum · Natrum silicicum ·
Natrum sulphuricum · Sulphur iodatum · Vespa vulgaris · Wyethia · Zincum phosphoricum

Those ten pages were **never archived by the Wayback Machine** — they return nothing, not
a throttle. They are missing rather than lost in parsing, which is why they are named here
instead of being silently absent.

## Known limitations

- **Ten remedies absent**, as above.
- **Prose, not numbered symptoms** — each remedy is carried whole in `sections.general`.
- **No corroboration** beyond the transcription.
