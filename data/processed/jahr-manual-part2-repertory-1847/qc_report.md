## What it completes

The library held Jahr's **Part I** in two halves (A–I and I–Z). This is **Part II**, the
Repertory, which the title page calls "Homoeopathic Symptomatology, with Clinical Remarks".

Twenty-five chapters, taken from the book's own headings: General Internal Affections ·
Affections of the Skin and External Organs · Sleep · Febrile Affections · Moral Affections ·
Affections of the Head and Scalp · Eyes and Sight · Ears and Hearing · Nose and Smell ·
Face … through to the sexual organs and the extremities.

## How it was read

Each `CHAPTER N.` heading is followed by the chapter's title in capitals, and the text runs
between one chapter heading and the next. Nothing is subdivided further, because Jahr writes
continuous clinical prose with the remedies cited inline — "Ars. asa. bell, bryon. cham.
hep. led." — rather than in a graded column.

## Known limitations

- **Prose, not graded links.** The remedies sit inside Jahr's sentences and are not separated
  into rubric rows with grades, because the page does not set them that way. This volume
  therefore adds no rubric count.
- **OCR damage** in the prose is carried through, including in the cited abbreviations.
- **No corroboration** beyond the scan.
