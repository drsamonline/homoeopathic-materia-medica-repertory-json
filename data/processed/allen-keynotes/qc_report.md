## Why a transcription, not a scan

The Médi-T site carries this book as typed pages split into twelve files. A typed page has
no OCR damage, which is why the text here is cleaner than anything scanned in this library —
no "IIOERK'KE", no mangled remedy names.

## How the parse works

Every remedy begins at an anchor in the source pages. The remedy's name is the first
title-case line ending in a full stop; everything after it is its keynote text, kept
verbatim. A chunk with fewer than twenty words is dropped as a fragment rather than
published as a remedy.

## Known limitations

- **183 remedies**, against the roughly 200 the book carries. The gap is remedies whose
  anchor or name line the source itself does not carry; they are absent rather than
  reconstructed.
- **One source, no corroboration.** The fidelity claim is "this is what the transcription
  says".
