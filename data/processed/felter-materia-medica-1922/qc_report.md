| Licence of the TEXT | Public domain — Felter's 1922 text |

A remedy opens with its name set in capitals on a line of its own — `ACACIA.`,
`ACONITUM NEPALLUS.`, `ALLIUM CEPA.` — followed by its monograph. The caps rule finds 289
headings; three carried too little text and were dropped rather than published as stubs.

**Limitation:** each monograph is carried whole in `sections.general`, not split into
numbered symptoms, so this volume adds no `symptoms` count.

**Chapter titles are generic** — `Chapter I`, `Chapter II` — because the book's own titles
came out of the scan as debris ("'TT^HE limits of the curable and of"). The contents page
is equally damaged, so no title was invented to replace them. The body text is verbatim.

**Not built:** the supplied PDFs of this title and of Clarke's *Cold Catching* are
image-only scans — every body page carries no text layer. OCR of 231 and 142 pages is not
practical here, so Burnett was rebuilt from the archive's text instead, and Clarke's
*Cold Catching* is held back until a text-bearing copy turns up.
