## What it is

Kent's fugitive pieces — "A criticism of Dr. Holmes", "A Study In Materia Medica", "Why is
cancer incurable?", "The relation of gout to the voluntary system", "Tuberculosis", and
thirty more. Each is a separate essay with its own title, taken from the numbered heading the
transcription prints.

## Coverage — stated exactly

**Thirty-six essays are here.** A further thirty pages of the same transcription were **not
fetched**: the Wayback Machine served 39 of 68 pages in this session before throttling, and
the remaining pages exist but would not come. They can be added later without rework — this
is a throttle, not a permanent absence, which is the opposite of the situation with Kent's
*New Remedies*, where ten remedies were never archived at all.

## Known limitations

- **Partial coverage**, as above.
- **Titles are the transcription's own** — some are terse ("Lecture", "Address") because that
  is how the page heads them, and inventing fuller titles would be invention.
- **No corroboration** beyond the transcription.
