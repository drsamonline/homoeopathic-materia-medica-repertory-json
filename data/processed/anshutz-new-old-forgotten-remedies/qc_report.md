## What this is

A collection of papers by many writers, each on a remedy: the natural order, common names,
then the writers' clinical accounts. It is carried as a `treatise` with one section per
remedy, because the papers are prose by different hands and do not share a symptom schema.

## How the parse works

A paper begins where its remedy is printed in capitals. The book's own *List of Remedies*
is the index and is not carried as a paper; neither are its sub-headings ("Clinical Cases"),
which belong inside a remedy's paper rather than beside it.

## Known limitations

- **Transcription, not a scan**, so the text is cleaner than any OCR — but it is a
  transcription, and its own conventions (ligatures, small caps, spacing) are Gutenberg's.
- **One source, no corroboration.** There is no second copy to check against.
- A paper's internal sub-headings are folded into its text.
