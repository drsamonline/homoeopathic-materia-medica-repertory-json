## Structure

This volume is a chaptered prose work, not a remedy-by-remedy materia medica and not a
book of numbered paragraphs, so it is carried as a `treatise` whose sections have a
`title` instead of a paragraph number. The chapter list is taken from the book's own
printed INDEX, which gives titles with page ranges; the scan's page numbers equal its PDF
page numbers, so a range maps straight onto pages.

Where the index nests a subsection inside a chapter — "Gonorrhoea 132–162" containing
"Ways of infection 138–141", for instance — each section is clipped at the next section's
first page. Nothing is printed twice, and no text is dropped between sections.

## Known limitations

- **No second source.** One scan of this printing exists here, so the text cannot be
  corroborated against another copy the way the Organon volumes were. The claim is that
  this is what the scan's own text layer says, not that two scans agree.
- **The scan's OCR damage is carried through**, not corrected: "sycOSIS", "thereapeutic",
  "sub ject" and similar. Correcting them would be rewriting.
- **The printing is undated.** The work was first published in 1908; this printing's text
  cites a case dated 1906, so it is the 1908 edition or a later printing. The year is
  recorded as 1908 on that basis and the caveat is kept in the book record.
- **A correction.** An earlier version of this record said Allen died in 1900. He died on
  1 August 1925; the public-domain claim is unchanged, since the US term on a 1908
  publication ran out in 2004.
- 0 chapter(s) skipped after clipping.
