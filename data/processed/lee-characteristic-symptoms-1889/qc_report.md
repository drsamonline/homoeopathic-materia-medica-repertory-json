## This is a fragment, and it says so

**Two chapters only**: Mind and Disposition, and Symptoms of the Head. Lee's repertory runs
to some forty chapters; this scan carries the first two. No other scan of the title exists on
the archive — the search returns only Gentry's Concordance and Clarke's Clinical Repertory —
so the rest cannot be recovered from here.

The volume is published rather than withheld because the 5,699 rubrics in it are real,
verbatim and useful; but the title and the catalogue note both say "Mind and Head only", and
the record carries a `coverage_note` so no reader mistakes it for the whole book.

## How it was read

The printed form is `rubric: remedy. remedy. remedy.` — "Abandoned, feels as if: carb-an.
eye. hura. k-ca. lam. stram." The rubric is the text before the colon; the remedies are the
abbreviations after it.

**Remedies are kept as printed**, in `abbrev`, with `name` left null. Lee's abbreviations are
two or three letters and cannot be resolved without guessing — and a guessed name attached to
a symptom is worse than the printed token the book actually gives.

## Known limitations

- **Two of ~40 chapters**, as above.
- **No grades** — none are printed in this edition.
- **OCR damage** in rubric text and abbreviations carried through.
